About CEPOH Rwanda

The Center for Emerging Pathogens and One Health (CEPOH Rwanda) is a nationally registered scientific research and technical consultancy center based in Kigali, Rwanda. The center focuses on emerging infectious diseases, zoonotic risks, environmental health, and the One Health approach integrating human, animal, and ecosystem health.

Our Mission

To provide cutting-edge research, training, and consultancy services that strengthen national and regional capacity to prevent, detect, and respond to emerging pathogens and health threats.

Core Activities

  • Scientific research on emerging and re-emerging pathogens
  • One Health capacity building initiatives
  • Technical consultancy and laboratory support
  • Disease surveillance and outbreak investigation
  • Community health and environmental health programs

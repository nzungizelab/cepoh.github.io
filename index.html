About CEPOH Rwanda

The Center for Emerging Pathogens and One Health (CEPOH Rwanda) is a nationally registered scientific research and technical consultancy center based in Kigali, Rwanda. We focus on emerging infectious diseases, zoonotic diseases, environmental health, and the One Health approach integrating human, animal, and environmental health.

Mission

To strengthen national and regional capacity to prevent, detect, and respond to emerging pathogens through research, training, and One Health systems.
